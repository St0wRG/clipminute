1. What ClipMinute is

ClipMinute is a desktop application for independent short-form video creators. It generates short vertical videos (script, voice-over, subtitles, stock background footage) and lets each creator publish those videos to the TikTok account they personally own and connect. ClipMinute is distributed to creators who install it on their own computers; each creator uses it to manage only the account they connect through TikTok Login.

2. Acceptable use

Creators must use ClipMinute in compliance with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Each creator is responsible for ensuring that the content they publish is either originally generated or based on properly licensed material (royalty-free footage and music, with attribution where required), and for disclosing commercial or branded content when applicable.

3. TikTok integration

Publishing to TikTok is performed through the official TikTok Content Posting API, with each creator's explicit consent granted via TikTok Login. On the publish screen the creator reviews the video, sets the caption, selects the privacy level (from the options their account allows), toggles interaction settings, and — when relevant — discloses commercial content, before confirming the post. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings.

4. Accounts and data

Each creator connects and manages their own account only. ClipMinute does not aggregate, resell, or display other users' TikTok content. Data handling is described in our Privacy Policy.

5. Liability

The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Each creator is solely responsible for the content they publish through ClipMinute.
